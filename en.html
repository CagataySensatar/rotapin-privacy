Rotapin · Privacy Policy

Türkçe

Last updated: October 18, 2026

In short: everything you enter in Rotapin (injection logs, doses, finger-prick measurements, glucose values, notes, profile details) is stored only on your phone. There is no account and no server; this data is never sent to us or anyone else. Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports are off by default and are only sent if you turn them on.

1. What the app does

Rotapin is a logging app that helps you rotate insulin injection sites and keep track of which finger you used for finger-prick blood tests. It does not calculate or suggest doses and does not interpret glucose values. It is not medical advice; consult your doctor about your treatment.

2. Data stored on your device

The following is kept, only as far as you enter it, in a private database inside the app on your phone:

  • Injection logs: date and time, body site and point, insulin name/type, (optional) dose, tags and note.
  • Finger-prick measurements: date and time, finger and side, (optional) glucose value, timing tag and note.
  • Insulin and pen information (pen opening date, usable period).
  • Profile (all optional): name or nickname, gender, year of birth, diabetes type, year of diagnosis.
  • Settings and basal alarm times.

This data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Uninstalling the app or using Settings → Delete all data removes all of it from the device.

3. Optional: anonymous usage statistics and crash reports

To improve the app, Google's Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics are used only if you give explicit consent under Settings → Privacy (or in the first-launch dialog). Consent is off by default and can be withdrawn at any time in the same place.

4. Notifications and permissions

  • Notifications: pen reminders and the basal alarm are scheduled entirely on your phone; no push notifications are used.
  • Alarms & reminders / full-screen notifications: so the basal alarm rings on time and shows on the lock screen.
  • Run at startup: to re-schedule alarms after the phone restarts.
  • File picker: only when you choose to restore a backup, to read the file you select.

5. Files you export

Backup (JSON), spreadsheet (CSV) and doctor report (PDF) files are created only when you ask and are sent, through your phone's share sheet, to the destination you choose. These files contain health data; be careful where you send them. Once shared, the file is subject to the terms of the service you sent it to.

6. Children

Users under 16 should use the app under the supervision of a parent or guardian. Optional statistics consent for users under 16 should be given by a parent or guardian.

7. Your rights

You can exercise your rights under the GDPR and Turkish data protection law (KVKK). Because your personal health data is only on your device, you manage it directly (view, edit, export, delete). For requests about the optional statistics data, use the contact channel below.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the current version is published on this page and the date above is updated.
